Meet the Doctors

The team behind every appointment

Two licensed veterinarians, one shared belief: every pet deserves to feel safe the moment they walk through the door.

Dr. Mitchell

DVM · Oklahoma State University, 2006

Born and raised in the Guymon area, Dr. Mitchell practiced in Stillwater for 14 years before returning home to be closer to family. She's trained in the Fear Free Method and believes every pet should feel like they're "coming to grandma's house" — spoiled rotten, and never wanting to leave.

Fear Free Certified Wellness & Surgery

Dr. Mondragon-Evans

DVM · UNAM, Mexico City, 2007

Originally from Mexico City, Dr. Mondragon-Evans is fully bilingual in English and Spanish and has a special interest in the care and treatment of exotic pets and wildlife — so if your household is a little more unusual than most, she's genuinely excited to meet it.

Bilingual (EN/ES) Exotic & Wildlife Care
